Privacy Policy

Ghar ka Khana respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website to browse the menu or place an order, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account or place an order, we may collect your name, phone number, email address (if provided), delivery address, order details, and any notes you choose to provide.

How we use information

We use this information to create and manage accounts, confirm and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, respond to questions, provide order-related updates, and maintain the security and operation of our service.

WhatsApp and service providers

We may use WhatsApp and its business messaging services to receive or send order-related communications. Information processed through WhatsApp is also subject to Meta's applicable terms and privacy policy. We use hosting, database, and technical service providers to operate the website; they process information as needed to provide those services.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to process your order, operate our service, comply with law, or protect our rights and users.

Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect personal information. We retain it for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfilment, customer service, record-keeping, and legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it where appropriate.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to information we must retain for legal or legitimate business purposes.

Children's privacy

Our website is intended for people placing food orders and is not directed to children. Please do not submit a child's personal information without appropriate consent.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact
