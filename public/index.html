Our Story

A Home Kitchen with a Big Dream

Ghar ka Khana started with a simple thought – to bring the warmth and comfort of homemade food to more people. What began as a small home kitchen, cooking with love for family and friends, has grown into a cloud kitchen serving wholesome, delicious meals to many happy customers.

Read More ⌄

For us, cooking has always been about more than putting a meal on the table. It is about the familiar aroma of spices, the joy of sharing food, and the little comforts that make an ordinary day feel special.

We prepare our food with thoughtful ingredients, care and attention to the flavours we grew up loving. From everyday homely meals and traditional favourites to lighter, healthier choices, our aim is to make good food feel personal and welcoming.

Every order is a reminder of why we began: to share a little taste of home, one meal at a time. Thank you for being part of the Ghar ka Khana family.
