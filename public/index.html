Our Story

A Home Kitchen with a Big Dream

Ghar ka Khana began in 2025 in my home kitchen in Bengaluru with a simple thought: to bring the warmth and comfort of homemade food to more people. I started by cooking for family and friends, who kept asking for more. Today, we serve families across the city with the same recipes, love and care that inspired us from the beginning.

From litti chokha and paneer gravies to light, healthy everyday meals, we prepare everything fresh with thoughtful ingredients, less oil and no preservatives—just the way we like to eat at home.

For me, cooking has always been about more than putting a meal on the table. It is the familiar aroma of spices, the joy of sharing food and the little comforts that make an ordinary day feel special. I bring the flavours I grew up loving into every dish, with care and attention so each meal feels personal, wholesome and welcoming.

Every order is a little taste of home. Thank you for being part of the Ghar ka Khana family.

Shipra Santosh
Founder

What began as a small home kitchen has grown into a cloud kitchen serving wholesome, delicious meals to many happy customers. Whether you are looking for a comforting everyday meal, a traditional favourite or a lighter, healthier choice, our hope is to make you feel at home with every bite.

Every recipe carries the same intention we started with: honest food, familiar flavours and the warmth of a meal made with love.

FAQ

How do I place an order?

Browse Order Food, choose a category, add your favourite dishes to the basket, review your order, select your saved delivery address and place the order.

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. Please sign up or log in before placing an order so that your order details, saved addresses and order history can be managed securely.

Can I schedule an order for later?

Yes, where scheduled ordering is available, you can select a date and exact delivery time during checkout.

How is the delivery charge calculated?

Delivery charges are based on the delivery distance from our kitchen and are shown during checkout before you place the order. Orders within the applicable free-delivery area may have no delivery charge.

What payment options are available?

Available payment options are shown at checkout. Online payments are processed through the payment gateway, while Cash on Delivery is available only where the service is enabled.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Please contact us as soon as possible after placing the order. Once preparation has started, changes or cancellations may not be possible.

Are the food images exactly the same as the delivered food?

No. The dish images displayed on this website are AI-generated for illustrative and presentation purposes. They are intended to represent the dish and may not exactly match the appearance, portion presentation, garnish or plating of the food delivered.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

  • Information you voluntarily provide through reviews, enquiries or support requests.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process, prepare and deliver your orders.
  • To communicate with you about orders, payments, delivery and support.

Payments

Sharing of information

Data security & retention

We take reasonable measures to protect customer information. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfilment, customer support, business records, fraud prevention and legal or regulatory requirements.

Your choices

You may contact us to request help with your account information or to ask questions about how your information is used. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

1. Orders

Orders are accepted subject to item availability, delivery-area availability and the information provided by the customer. An order is confirmed only after the applicable confirmation or payment process is completed.

2. Menu & food images

Menu items, ingredients, availability and prices may change without prior notice. All dish images shown on the website are AI-generated illustrative images and are not photographs of every actual serving. The delivered food may differ from the displayed image in appearance, colour, garnish, portion presentation and plating.

3. Food preparation

Food is prepared fresh according to the selected order. Customers should inform us of relevant food preferences or allergies before ordering. We cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

4. Delivery

Delivery is available only within the serviceable area. Delivery charges, where applicable, are calculated according to the applicable delivery-distance rules and are displayed before order confirmation. Delays may occur because of traffic, weather, operational conditions or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

5. Scheduled orders

Scheduled orders are subject to the selected date and time being available. Please ensure someone is available to receive the order at the selected delivery time.

6. Payments

Customers must provide accurate payment and contact information. Online payments are subject to the terms of the payment provider. Failed, cancelled or incomplete payments may result in an order not being confirmed.

7. Cancellation & changes

Requests to cancel or modify an order should be made as early as possible. Once food preparation or dispatch has started, cancellation or changes may not be possible.

8. Refunds

Where a refund is applicable, it will be handled according to the circumstances of the order and the payment method used. Any applicable refund timeline may depend on the payment provider or banking system.

9. Customer responsibility

Customers are responsible for providing a correct delivery address, phone number and other order information. Additional delays or delivery difficulties caused by incorrect information may not be the responsibility of Ghar ka Khana.

10. Contact & support

For questions, order issues or support, please contact Ghar ka Khana through the support/contact option provided on the website.
