Our Story

A Home Kitchen with a Big Dream

Ghar ka Khana began in 2025 in my home kitchen in Bengaluru with a simple thought: to bring the warmth and comfort of homemade food to more people. I started by cooking for family and friends, who kept asking for more. Today, we serve families across the city with the same recipes, love and care that inspired us from the beginning.

From litti chokha and paneer gravies to light, healthy everyday meals, we prepare everything fresh with thoughtful ingredients, less oil and no preservatives—just the way we like to eat at home.

For me, cooking has always been about more than putting a meal on the table. It is the familiar aroma of spices, the joy of sharing food and the little comforts that make an ordinary day feel special. I bring the flavours I grew up loving into every dish, with care and attention so each meal feels personal, wholesome and welcoming.

Every order is a little taste of home. Thank you for being part of the Ghar ka Khana family.

Shipra Santosh
Founder

What began as a small home kitchen has grown into a cloud kitchen serving wholesome, delicious meals to many happy customers. Whether you are looking for a comforting everyday meal, a traditional favourite or a lighter, healthier choice, our hope is to make you feel at home with every bite.

Every recipe carries the same intention we started with: honest food, familiar flavours and the warmth of a meal made with love.
